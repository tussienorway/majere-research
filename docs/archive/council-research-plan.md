# Research plan

## Architecture
Council of archetypes → shared affect state (valence/arousal) → action. The impulsivity parameter (number of council rounds, veto threshold) controls how much deliberation happens before acting.

## Questions and hypotheses
| RQ | Hypothesis | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | The affect state improves consistency and appropriateness | Cross-episode consistency; human- and LLM-judged appropriateness |
| RQ2 | Impulsivity yields a monotone speed–quality trade-off | Accuracy vs. latency/tokens curve |
| RQ3 | The council improves calibration and reduces contradictions | ECE, abstention precision, self-contradiction rate |
| RQ4 | Functional indicators are present and depend on specific components | Indicator presence with and without ablations |

RQ4 is about indicators only, not claims of consciousness or feelings.

## Timeline
- 0–3 months: pilot
- 3–6 months: full evaluation and a public demo
- 6–12 months: preprint, plus open code and data
